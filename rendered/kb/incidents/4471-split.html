  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April goes out as 4 July. Reports by app, by email, and by the booking-v2 form, whose hint says DD/MM/YYYY.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type month first, as told, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different place: a parser fix does not touch it. Retiring the form ends it.
  • shift: no swap at all. The date moves by one day (“Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”), or the invite arrives before the booking, or two attendees in two time zones get two different days. The desk had read these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json) and still counted them under the parser.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match, without reading the body. Every one of these reports says “wrong day” in some form, so all three problems landed in one ticket. The subject tells you that a date is wrong, not why. Intake’s tag does not help either: it is guessed from the reporter’s browser, and the table counts how often it disagrees with the tenant.

For the desk

  • Before merging into a big ticket, read a sample of bodies. A swap (4 July for 7 April) and a one-day move (Monday for Tuesday) are different bugs with the same subject line.
  • Place a report by its tenant (locale and booking form), not by intake’s tag.
  • Decision 0001 needs a successor record: subject match proposes a merge, a person confirms it.
